# Services card image briefs

These service illustrations were generated with the built-in image generation tool. They are illustrative scenes, not photographs of Enlace employees or client systems. The generated masters were converted to WebP for the website without changing the compositions.

## Shared prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create one premium editorial photograph for an Enlace Global BPO service card. Landscape 16:9 composition, high resolution, clean contemporary Indian business operations office, soft natural daylight, restrained white, navy and subtle emerald-green palette. Realistic people, realistic hands and screens, approachable and professional. The service activity must be immediately recognizable at thumbnail size. Keep the important subject and device within the central 70% so a wide web card crop remains clear. Software interfaces should use simple visual structures and icons, not dense readable text or customer data. No company logos, brand names, watermarks, titles, decorative captions, floating holograms or sci-fi overlays. This is an illustrative stock-style service photograph, not a portrait of an actual Enlace employee.
